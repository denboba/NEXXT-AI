Concept · 12 / 17

The architecture is deliberately simple, and that is what lets it scale safely. On the left, the channels: the social ad, the web chat and the app. In the middle, the AI conversation layer — the coach. Its job is to ask, explain, answer worries and remember context. It works inside guardrails: approved topics, approved wording and never a promise of returns. On the right, the bank’s own systems, which the AI calls as tools: the simulation engine produces every number, eKYC and onboarding handle identity, core banking moves the money, and the product catalogue defines what can be offered. The language model never invents a figure and never moves money on its own. That separation is what makes compliance, audit and scale possible.
