This is the heart of the experience. Three questions — how much, what worries you, how long — and the visitor immediately sees their own numbers. At 500 lei a month for five years, they would put aside 30,000 lei. Kept as cash, its real value after inflation is about 26,379 lei. In a diversified fund at an assumed 7.2 percent a year, it could reach about 35,771 lei — roughly 9,392 lei better off. A slider lets the visitor try anything from 100 to 2,000 lei a month. Two things matter for growth here. First, personalisation: the numbers are theirs, not a brochure’s. Second, objection handling: one-tap follow-ups answer the exact questions that normally end a funnel. The numbers come from the bank’s own calculation engine, not from the language model.
