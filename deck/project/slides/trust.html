Trust by design

Six guardrails that make it safe to scale

1 · Bank’s own numbers

Every figure comes from the simulation engine, never from the model.

2 · Clear risk labels

“Simulation only”, risk level and fees shown next to every result.

3 · Suitability first

The regulated appropriateness check runs before any product is offered.

4 · Human on call

Complex questions go to an adviser in one tap, with the context attached.

5 · Fully auditable

Every conversation is logged and reviewable by compliance.

6 · Data protected

Explicit consent, GDPR-aligned handling, no data used beyond its purpose.

In banking, you cannot scale what you cannot trust. These six guardrails are design principles, not afterthoughts. One: every number comes from the bank’s simulation engine, not from the language model. Two: results always carry a clear simulation label, the risk level and the fees. Three: the regulated appropriateness or suitability check runs before any investment product is offered. Four: a human adviser is one tap away, and receives the full conversation so the customer does not repeat themselves. Five: every conversation is logged and can be reviewed by compliance. Six: data is handled with explicit consent and GDPR-aligned controls. Confirm the exact wording and regulatory references with your compliance team before presenting externally.
