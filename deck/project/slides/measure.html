We measure the whole journey as one funnel, from ad click to first debit. Five steps: the chat start rate after an ad click; how many complete the three questions and see a simulation; how many open an account or log in; how many activate a plan; and finally how many actually have a first debit executed. That last one is the north star, because a click or a sign-up is not growth — money invested is. Because every conversation is logged step by step, each drop-off points us to a specific thing to fix. On the business side we track the cost per first investment and how many plans are still active after twelve months.
