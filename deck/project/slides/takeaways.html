16 / 17

If you remember three things from this talk, make it these. One: put AI where your customers actually drop out of the journey, not where it makes the nicest demo. For us, that was the moment between seeing an ad and trusting a bank with savings. Two: let the AI do what it is good at — talking, explaining, remembering — and let your own regulated systems produce the numbers, check identity and move money. That is what makes it safe to scale. Three: measure all the way to the first debit. Clicks and chats are vanity metrics; money invested is growth.
