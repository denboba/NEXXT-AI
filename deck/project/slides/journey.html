Here is the whole journey on one slide. Step one, the hook: a social ad that makes the invisible loss visible. Step two, convince: a short chat in the browser — no download, no login — asks three questions and builds a live simulation with the visitor’s own numbers. Step three, convert: new visitors open an account in about three minutes; existing clients simply log in. Step four, activate: the same conversation continues inside the app and presents real products. Notice that AI plays a different role at every step — that is what makes it a growth engine rather than a chatbot bolted onto a website.
