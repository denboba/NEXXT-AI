Illustrative · 03 / 17

10,000 lei sitting in a current account, at roughly 5.5 percent inflation a year, buys only about 7,651 lei worth of goods after five years — a loss of around 23.5 percent of purchasing power. The problem is that this loss is invisible: the balance on the statement never goes down. That is exactly why people do not act on it. For a bank, this is a growth opportunity hiding in plain sight: customers who already trust us with deposits but have never invested. Figures are illustrative and assume constant inflation.
