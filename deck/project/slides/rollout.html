Draft plan · 15 / 17

Scaling an AI product in a regulated business is a staged process. Phase one is a pilot: one social channel and one product — the monthly fund plan — for both new and existing clients, with compliance reviewing every script. We move on only when the activation rate passes an agreed threshold. Phase two expands to all social channels and the full product shelf, and introduces weekly A/B tests of hooks and answers; the gate here is cost per first investment. Phase three is scale: the coach becomes available to every client in the app, with monthly check-ins, cross-sell and more languages or markets.
