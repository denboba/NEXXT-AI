Illustrative · 08 / 17

Conversion is where most digital funnels die, so we designed one button with two doors. New visitors open a free account in about three minutes: the details they already gave in the chat are pre-filled, they scan their ID and take a selfie for remote identification, and sign with an SMS code. Their virtual card is ready in Apple Pay or Google Pay right away. Existing clients simply approve a login on their phone. The key growth detail is continuity: the answers and the simulation travel with the person, so nobody has to start over. This also means the same flow works for acquisition of new customers and for activation of existing ones.
